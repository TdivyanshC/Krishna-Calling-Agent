# English Script Sample — for tone approval before the full translation

15 lines covering greeting → offer → top objections → close, plus the 2 new
lines the bilingual system needs. Not literal translations of the Hindi warm
rewrite — same content/intent, phrased the way an Indian English speaker
would naturally say it on a call. Audio previews (all in `shreya`, just as a
placeholder voice — real voice choice is still open) are in
`/mnt/user-data/outputs/english_script_sample/`.

Once you approve the tone here, I'll translate the remaining ~250 lines
(REACT_A/B/C's remaining objections/close variants, Call2/Call3, Fresh CTA,
the 22 situational replies) in this same register.

---

### Greeting / opener
1. **ra_greet_main equivalent** — Upgrading your home furniture has become really simple. We buy your old furniture at a good value, and give you a special discount on new pieces too. Just thirty seconds — can I quickly explain the offer?
2. **ra_greet_who equivalent** — Hi, this is Priya from Krishna Furniture. You've been a valued customer of ours before, so I wanted to reach out personally — there's a special offer just for you.

### Offer
3. **ra_offer_main equivalent** — Right now we have a 25% discount on new furniture, and when you exchange your old furniture, it gets its own separate value on top — so your overall cost comes down quite a bit. Should I tell you one more good part?
4. **ra_offer_explain equivalent** — It's quite simple, actually — you visit the showroom, and we calculate the value of your old furniture right in front of you. That value, plus 25%, plus another 25% on the new piece — so you're looking at 43 to 50% in total savings. No hidden conditions.
5. **ra_offer_trust equivalent** — That's a completely fair question — with so many calls these days, it's natural to feel a little skeptical. That's exactly why I'd say, come visit the showroom once and see for yourself — no commitment at all, just for your own peace of mind.

### Objections
6. **not_interested** — No problem at all — just take a look at the photos and prices on WhatsApp whenever you get a chance. No pressure. If you like what you see, we can take it from there.
7. **busy** — Totally understand, you're busy right now — I'll just send the details on WhatsApp. Take your time, look whenever it's convenient. No rush at all.
8. **expensive** — I understand, keeping an eye on the budget matters. That's exactly why this is an exchange offer — you're not paying for a brand new piece outright, it adjusts against your old furniture. I'll send the exact amount on WhatsApp so you can see for yourself how much it comes down to.

### Close / DNC / repeat
9. **ra_close equivalent** — Wonderful! I'll send the photos and prices on WhatsApp right away. And do drop by the showroom once — we'll take care of your old furniture, you just pick out the new one. See you soon, thank you!
10. **ra_dnc equivalent** — Of course, I'm really sorry for the trouble. I'll remove your number from our calling list right away — you won't get another call from us. Thank you so much, take care.
11. **obj_repeat_generic equivalent** — Oh, I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that — could you say that once more, please?

### New for the bilingual system
12. **Someone explicitly asks for English** (short, warm confirmation) — Sure, I can continue in English if that's easier for you.
13. **Genuine STT/language confusion — the clarifying question** (deliberately bilingual so it lands either way) — Sorry, just to check — would you like to continue in English, or in Hindi? Jo bhi aapko comfortable lage.
14. **want_human / escalate equivalent** — I'm the one handling this call right now, but I can have someone from our showroom team speak with you directly, or arrange a callback from our Customer Relations Head — whichever works better for you.
15. **wa_ok equivalent (short filler)** — Sure, sending that over on WhatsApp right now.
